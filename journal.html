You mean a lot

You.

Your dumb little rants that make you sound completely silly, like when you pretend to be a dog and jokingly bark just to be funny.

Your smile. The way it just brightens up your whole face, especially when you tilt your head a certain way.

I could honestly keep going forever, but I'll run out of pages, and I still have a lot more I want to write about. There are so many other things I cherish about you that aren't even on these pages.

Not because you're perfect.

Not because you've never made mistakes.

This year has been really hard on me with work, responsibilities, and everything else life was throwing at me. I let all of that cloud my depression and anxiety, and I let it take control of my own emotions. That's not an excuse for how I acted. It's just what was happening inside me, and I wish I had understood it better while I was going through it.

I never should have allowed what I was dealing with to affect you or our relationship the way it did.

I wanted so badly to build the perfect life for us. I wanted the perfect house. I wanted the perfect way for us to get married. I wanted us to have a life where compromises came easily instead of everything feeling like a struggle. I wanted to figure out how to take care of the animals, watch over Raven, visit both of our families, make time for each other, and somehow keep everything balanced and happy.

I wanted to be able to handle all of it.

Raven was a big part of that. I always felt bad asking someone else to watch her because I felt like she was my responsibility. I wanted her to be able to socialize with more people too, because I wanted to eventually be able to bring her with us instead of feeling like I had to stay tied down to that responsibility.

I pictured us having that freedom.

I didn't want to burden you with what was happening inside me because you had your own struggles, and I wanted to be the person who took care of you rather than becoming another thing you had to take care of.

But in trying not to burden you, I stopped communicating with you.

And that was part of the problem.

I kept things inside until they became too much. I let frustration build. I became bitter and cold. I let things affect how I treated you, even though you didn't deserve any of that.

I'm sorry.

I wish I had told you what was happening instead of trying so hard to carry it by myself.

I kept telling myself I just needed to push through a little longer because I believed things would eventually get better and that we'd finally have the life we'd been working toward. But I didn't realize how much I was drowning emotionally while I was trying to get there.

Even if you have to "Tortoise" me into doing it. lol.
